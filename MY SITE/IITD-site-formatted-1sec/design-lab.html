INSTITUTE PROFILE

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

IIT Delhi is a public technical institute focused on education, research and development in science, engineering and technology. This student guide summarizes information published by the institute; use the official pages linked below for complete and current details.

HISTORY

From the College of Engineering to IIT Delhi

The institute was established as the College of Engineering in 1961. It was declared an Institution of National Importance under the Institutes of Technology (Amendment) Act, 1963, and renamed the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. IIT Delhi later received the Institution of Eminence designation in 2018.

VISION

Education and research for society

The institute’s stated vision is to contribute to India and the world through excellence in scientific and technical education and research, serve industry and society, and remain a source of pride for India.

ACADEMICS & RESEARCH

Learning across disciplines

IIT Delhi offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral study, alongside research across engineering, sciences, management, design and humanities. Its departments, schools and centres support teaching and research, including work that brings different disciplines together.
