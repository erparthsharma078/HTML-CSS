RESEARCH

Research & discovery

IIT Delhi research spans engineering, science, healthcare, energy, data and public-impact work. Teams combine laboratories, interdisciplinary centres, industry partnerships and student research.

RESEARCH IMPACT REPORT · 2018–2024

A broad research ecosystem

In its January 2025 Research Impact Report, IIT Delhi reported more than 5,100 sponsored research projects with cumulative funding of ₹2,340 crore, and about 3,000 consultancy projects worth approximately ₹300 crore over the seven-year period.
